URUL

Landslide early warning demo for Wayanad district, Kerala. The same app as the Android APK, running in the browser.

  • Map: switch 24, 48 and 72 h; tap a coloured zone.
  • Alerts: open an alert, then Show on hazard map.
  • Roads: tap a road on the map or in the list.
  • Report: allow location when asked, or tap Type position (e.g. 11.5553, 76.1345). Take photo opens the camera on a phone, or a file picker on a laptop.
  • Simulate: pick a scenario or move the sliders; switch to District map to see the simulated hazard change, tap a place to load it, then Show on Hazard Map.
  • Without internet the street map is hidden and the map uses its offline view, as on a phone in flight mode.
DEMO DATA. NOT A REAL LANDSLIDE WARNING.
